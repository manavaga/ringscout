# RingScout Support

Need help with RingScout? This page explains where to ask questions, report defects and request privacy support.

## Get help

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** https://github.com/manavaga/ringscout/issues
- **Project and setup guide:** https://github.com/manavaga/ringscout
- **Live service:** https://ringscout-hom7ntreuq-uc.a.run.app/

For a useful support request, include:
1. What you were trying to find out (for example, table availability, a clinic slot or a direct tariff).
2. The city and locality used.
3. The approximate time and date of the request, including time zone.
4. The category and, if relevant, the business name.
5. The error message or a short description of the unexpected result.
6. A request or trace identifier, if the client provided one.

Please redact personal phone numbers, access tokens, payment details and other sensitive information. Do not post call audio or transcripts publicly unless you have the right to share them and have removed personal information.

## Common issues

### A business did not answer
RingScout depends on the business answering its listed phone number. A busy line, unanswered call, IVR, incorrect listing or network issue may prevent a useful response. Try another business or retry later.

### The price or availability was different
A call reports what the business communicated at the time. Prices, slots, stock and availability can change. Confirm directly with the business before travelling, paying or relying on a quote.

### The business says it did not receive a call
Check the date, approximate time and locality. Calls may fail or be routed through an IVR. Share the request identifier privately through an appropriate support channel, not in a public issue.

### Audio proof will not play
Try downloading the audio file and opening it in a standard WAV-compatible player. Do not share audio publicly if it contains personal information or a business representative's voice without a lawful basis.

Please do not report exploitable vulnerabilities in public issues. Follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Response expectations

RingScout is an independently operated project. There is currently no guaranteed response time or service-level agreement. Support availability may vary.
